# Rules for tax year 2025 (return filed in 2026)

Researched 14 October 2026. Primary source: the Tax Department's announcement of
18 June 2026 and its "Guide for completing the Individual Income Tax Return 2025"
(summarised field by field in `official-guide-2025.md`). These rules apply **only to
tax year 2025**. If the tax year being filed is not 2025, tell the user this file is out
of date and check the Tax Department's current guidance first.

Use this file to check the user's data and the calculation — not to give tax advice.
When something is unclear for the user's situation, say so and suggest a tax advisor.

## Portal

- Tax years up to 2025 are filed **only in TAXISnet** (taxisnet.mof.gov.cy).
- From tax year 2026 the personal return moves to **Tax For All (TFA)**.
- Personal details, representative and the refund IBAN are changed **only in TFA**
  (forms T.D.2003 and T.D.2008), not in the return.

## Deadline and penalties (official announcement, 18 June 2026)

- Filing **and** paying tax due for 2025: **31 October 2026** (decree K.D.P. 260/2026).
  Paid by then — no interest or charges.
- Late filing: **€150** charge (art. 50A(a), Law 4/1978).
- Tax paid late: interest and charges apply.
- If today is past the deadline, say so plainly; the return can still be filed.

## Who must file

- Employees, pensioners and self-employed persons whose **gross** 2025 income exceeds
  **€19,500** (decree K.D.P. 52/2025). Gross means before deductions, so a return may be
  required even when no tax is due.
- Below the threshold filing is optional, but needed e.g. to get the automatic refund
  of SDC withheld on deposit interest (total income up to €12,000).
- Systematic trading/business income, even alongside a salary, requires the
  self-employed return (T.D.1 Self-employed) — this skill covers the ordinary T.D.1.

## Income tax bands 2025 (chargeable income)

| Band (€) | Rate | Tax in band (€) | Cumulative (€) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 – 19,500 | 0% | 0 | 0 |
| 19,501 – 28,000 | 20% | 1,700 | 1,700 |
| 28,001 – 36,300 | 25% | 2,075 | 3,775 |
| 36,301 – 60,000 | 30% | 7,110 | 10,885 |
| 60,001 and above | 35% | | |

Do **not** use the 2026 bands (0% up to €22,000 etc.).

## Deductions (Part 5)

**Overall cap of 1/5 of net income** for Part 5Γ items combined:

- pension / provident fund contributions (approved funds);
- Social Insurance contributions (Cyprus, or foreign under similar law — code 6);
- life insurance premiums — relief per policy limited to **7% of the sum insured**;
  spouse's life only for policies started up to 31/12/2002;
- medical funds and **private health insurance** (as medical fund, not life insurance)
  — limited to **2% of gross income**;
- widows' pension fund.

GHS contributions are **not** entered as a deduction; the GHS calculation passes them
to the tax calculation automatically.

**Outside the cap (Part 5A)**, with receipts kept: trade union subscriptions;
professional subscriptions only if needed to earn the declared income; donations;
political party contributions up to €50,000; professional liability insurance of
employees; commission expenses of employees; and a few special cases (public-sector pay
reduction, community leaders, customs officers, "Renovate–Rent" scheme).

**Not available for 2025** (from 1 January 2026 only) — do not enter them even if the
user declared them on a 2026 T.D.59 for payroll: dependent children; home loan interest
or rent for the primary residence; home insurance against natural disasters;
energy-efficiency upgrades and electric vehicles; donations to cultural institutions.

## Exemptions for people who moved to Cyprus

Chosen through the **employment code** in Part 4A1 plus Part 4A2 — not in Part 5.
Only one applies. Collect the facts (start date of employment in Cyprus, years of
non-residence, prior work abroad, pay) and let the user confirm the code; if unsure,
suggest checking with the employer or an advisor.

- **Code 14 — 20%, max €8,550/year**: first employment in Cyprus started from
  26/07/2022, after at least 3 consecutive years working abroad for a non-resident
  employer; 7 years starting the year after employment began.
- **Code 3 — 20%, max €8,550/year**: employment started up to 25/07/2022, non-resident
  in the year before; 5 years.
- **Code 13 — 50%**: employment from 30/06/2023 (retroactive to 2022), not tax resident
  and no employment in Cyprus for 15 consecutive years before; applies in each year pay
  from employment in Cyprus exceeds **€55,000**; up to 17 years. Transitional cases for
  earlier starts.
- **Code 12 — 50%**: employment started 01/01/2022–29/06/2023 after at least 10 years
  of non-residence; pay over €55,000; up to 17 years; plus transitional cases.
- **Code 6 — 50%** (old rule): pay over €100,000, employment started up to 25/07/2022;
  10 years.
- **25% talent repatriation (art. 8(21B))** — claimed in Part 5A: max €25,000/year for
  7 years; not resident for the 7 years before starting work in Cyprus but resident at
  some earlier time; start between 2025 and 2030; pay over €30,000 (first 12 months and
  each year claimed); plus a foreign-employment condition (36 of 84 months with a
  recognised degree, or the full 84 months).

## Employment abroad / remote work

- Remote work for a foreign employer while living in Cyprus is **taxable in Cyprus**
  (code 1, or an exemption code).
- Work physically abroad for a non-resident employer (or a foreign PE) for more than 90
  days in the year is exempt (code 4).

## Rent

- Enter **gross** rent; 20% is deducted automatically for buildings (not for land,
  parking or "other").
- Capital allowance on the building cost (without land): 3% for offices, shops, flats,
  houses; 4% warehouses, factories, hotels.
- Interest on a loan to buy or build the rented property is fully deductible.
- **SDC**: 3% on 75% of gross rent (effective 2.25%), paid half-yearly; abolished only
  from 2026. **GHS**: 2.65% on gross rent.
- Short-term rentals (Airbnb, Booking…) are declared in Part 4Θ, not as rent: no SDC,
  GHS 2.65% (or 4%).

## Interest and dividends

- **Not subject to income tax**, but to SDC and GHS. Residents declare Cyprus and
  foreign amounts, even if exempt.
- SDC on interest: 17% (bank deposits, loans, foreign), 3% (government / corporate
  bonds). SDC on dividends: 17%. From 2026 the dividend rate falls to 5% — not for 2025.
- GHS: 2.65%.
- Foreign interest/dividends: no SDC withheld at source, so SDC becomes payable in the
  return (for domiciled residents). Foreign tax paid is credited up to the Cyprus tax.
- SDC withheld on deposit interest is refunded automatically if total income, including
  interest, is not more than **€12,000** — only if Part 4Ε is filled in.

## Non-domiciled residents (SDC)

- Non-doms are exempt from SDC but **not** from GHS, and still declare the income.
- If the user filed form **T.D.38** (non-dom declaration), the SDC calculation needs
  the exemption years filled in and the SDC income and deduction amounts deleted.
- A person resident in Cyprus for at least 17 of the last 20 years is deemed domiciled.

## GHS (ΓεΣΥ)

- 2.65% on employment, pension, rent, interest, dividends and other income; 4% on
  self-employed profit. Income capped at **€180,000** a year.
- Any GHS balance on line 10B, even 1 cent, becomes payable.
- Insured in another EEA country / Switzerland: GHS exemption via Part 3B2 (S1, A1 or
  other certificate).

## Residency

- **183-day rule**: more than 183 days in Cyprus in 2025.
- **60-day rule**: at least 60 days in Cyprus; permanent home owned or rented in
  Cyprus; not more than 183 days and not tax resident in any other country; business,
  employment or office in Cyprus not terminated by 31/12/2025.
- Residents declare **worldwide** income; non-residents only Cyprus income.

## Payment and refunds

- After the **final** submission, amounts due appear in the **Tax Portal**
  (taxportal.mof.gov.cy) → statement of amounts due, under payment codes (0300 income
  tax; 0313–0316 GHS; 0604/0612/0613 SDC). The user pays there.
- Refunds go to the IBAN on file; it can only be changed in Tax For All (T.D.2008).
  Refunds can be offset against other amounts owed.
