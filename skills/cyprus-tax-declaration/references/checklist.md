# Checklist: what to ask the user

Ask in one or two messages, in the user's language, only about what applies. Do not
ask in chat for passwords, card numbers, IBANs or other account numbers, Social
Insurance numbers, dates of birth or ID / passport numbers — the user types those into
the form themselves and Claude only checks that the fields are filled. Figures the
return needs (amounts, employer TICs) are fine to collect.

## Before starting

- Do you have the **T.D.63 (IR63)** certificate of emoluments from **every** employer
  you had in the tax year? How many employers were there? From each: employer TIC and
  name, months worked, gross pay, tax withheld, GHS withheld, Social Insurance and
  provident fund contributions, union subscriptions, benefits in kind.
- Do you receive a pension from Social Insurance or the Treasury, or are you registered
  as self-employed? (Decides the first-screen retrieval.)
- Were you a Cyprus tax resident in the year (183-day or 60-day rule)?
- When did you move to Cyprus and start your first job here? Were you working abroad
  for a foreign employer before? Is your pay over €55,000? (Decides codes 3/6/12/13/14
  or the 25% scheme.) Did your employer already apply an exemption in payroll?
- Are you non-domiciled, and did you file form T.D.38?
- Are you insured for health in another EEA country or Switzerland (S1 / A1)?

## Other income for the year

- Pension (Cyprus or foreign — for foreign: reduced 5% rate or normal rates?).
- Long-term rent (Cyprus or abroad): per property — title number, type, acquisition
  date and building cost without land, area, your share, tenant (person or company),
  gross rent, loan interest, foreign tax, SDC/GHS withheld by a company tenant.
- Short-term rentals (Airbnb, Booking…).
- Interest and dividends, Cyprus and foreign: per bank/company — gross amount, SDC and
  GHS withheld, foreign tax, date received.
- Work done physically abroad, foreign employment income, tax paid abroad (with proof).
- Self-employment or trading income (if systematic, the self-employed return applies).
- Non-taxable allowances (maternity/paternity, minimum guaranteed income) — declared
  for information.
- Surrender of a life insurance policy within 6 years of its start.

## Deductions (2025 rules)

- Life insurance: insurer, policy date, whose life, sum insured, premiums paid.
- Private health insurance / medical funds (not GHS).
- Provident / pension fund contributions not already on T.D.63.
- Trade union and necessary professional subscriptions not reimbursed.
- Donations and political party contributions (receipts).
- Foreign social insurance contributions while working in Cyprus.

Remind the user that children, home loan interest / rent, home insurance, energy
efficiency and EV deductions start only from tax year 2026.

## Personal details and refunds

- Address, email, representative and the refund IBAN cannot be changed in the return —
  only in Tax For All (forms T.D.2003 / T.D.2008). If the IBAN shown is wrong or
  missing, tell the user to update it there.
